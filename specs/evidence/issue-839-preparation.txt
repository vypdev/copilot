SDD Q3: Missing required text patches must be recovered before any framed-profile reviewer call through a dedicated trusted-host, read-only local Git source port. No unbounded provider fallback, silent placeholder-as-reviewed, hidden fetch or changed AI ignores. SDD Q4: Bind framed manifests and every assignment/receipt to the protocol/profile version, a fresh invocation nonce, repository identity, canonical open PR number and base/head repository ownership/ref identity, immutable base SHA, head SHA, unique merge-base SHA, ordered complete source manifest digest (including path/status/counts and source-content digests), ordered fragment coverage and relevant review context/configuration digest. Changed/missing base or head, closed/reowned/retargeted PR, changed source/assignment/context digest, nonce or profile mismatch invalidates the entire in-memory result and prevents publication. Treat failed identity reads as unverified, never current. Extended-profile support cannot weaken existing ownership, dismissal, protected approval or source coverage gates.
SDD Q5: Within one invocation only, preserve completed fully validated frame results while executing later frames under the identical source/context manifest; apply the existing bounded3 attempts only to the same failed partition assignment, without reusing an output that failed schema/identity validation. Do not resume after a whole-run provider/model interruption: discard and restart a fresh complete plan. Require exact ordered frame and partition identity/digest attestations, singleton schema bindings, one receipt per assigned fragment/partition, no duplicate/missing/corrupt/replayed/out-of-order substitutions, independent final global coverage and payload-budget reconciliation, and a final canonical identity re-read. A complete late frame may add findings and must participate in global normalization/deduplication/severity/comment-limit handling. Keep only the single global owner authorized to explicitly re-adjudicate retained prior finding IDs; omission is never resolution. No per-frame SCM writes, no intermediate clean result, no approval based on partial coverage, and no automatic reuse across invocations.
